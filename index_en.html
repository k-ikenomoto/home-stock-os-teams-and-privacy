HomeStockOS — Home Stock Management

Privacy Policy

日本語
HomeStockOS ("the App") is an Android app that helps you manage your household stock. This policy explains what information and permissions the App uses, and for what purposes.

1. App overview

The App lets you manage household stock such as food and daily necessities by photo and storage location, so you can quickly decide what to buy or not buy while shopping.

All data is stored on your device only. No internet connection is required, and no data is ever sent to the cloud.

2. Information we collect and use

  • Camera Used to take photos of your stock items. Photos are stored only in the app's private storage on your device and are never sent externally.
  • Notifications Used to show reminders for items with an expiry date or low/no stock. Notifications are generated entirely on your device and no data is sent to any external service.
  • Photos & stock information Used to display and manage the photos, names, expiry dates, and stock levels you register, entirely on your device.
Any information collected is used only for the purposes described above.

3. Where data is stored

Photos and stock information saved in the App are stored only in the app's private storage area on your device.

There is currently no feature that sends this information to any external server. All features work fully offline, without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

4. Sharing with third parties

The App never shares the photos or stock information you save with third parties. No sharing

We do not disclose your information to any third party, except where required by law.

5. Advertising & tracking

The App does not display any advertising. No ads

We do not track your behavior, location, or device identifiers in any way. No tracking

6. Data retention and deletion

When you delete a stock item, its related photo and information are immediately removed from the app's database.

Uninstalling the App deletes all data stored on your device. Backup and restore features are not currently provided.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as we add features or as laws change. If there is a significant change, we will let you know alongside an app update.

The latest version of this policy is always available on this page.
